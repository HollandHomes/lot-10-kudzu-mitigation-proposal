Project Conditions & Terms

Burn Safety & Extension Guidelines

  • Fire-Safety Compliance: All controlled burning will be conducted strictly in accordance with applicable NC fire-safety regulations, burn permits, weather conditions, and HOA requirements.
  • Extension Agent Plan: Scope follows the 4-point mitigation strategy established with the Extension Agent (limb clearance, flat area clearing, vine pulling/digging, and stump-cutting/marking).
  • Multi-Visit Eradication Guarantee: Includes two dedicated return trips by contractor—once in Spring and once in Early Summer—to inspect marked roots and treat any new shoots.

Financial Cap & HOA Parameters

  • Hard Cost Cap Protection: The total flat-rate investment of $700.00 covers all initial work and both seasonal follow-ups. No additional expense beyond the $750.00 maximum cap will ever be incurred without prior written approval.
  • Ongoing HOA Maintenance: Following completion of contractor's initial clearing and seasonal eradication visits, the HOA commits to monitor and mow the cleared area through volunteer efforts at no additional cost to the lot owner.
  • Proposal Expiration: All values listed in this proposal are guaranteed for 30 calendar days from the date of issue.
